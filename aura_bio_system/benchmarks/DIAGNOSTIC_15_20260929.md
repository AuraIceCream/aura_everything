# Fixed 15-question diagnostic (2026-09-29)

This is a **purposive failure-analysis slice**, not an unbiased estimate of AURA's overall accuracy. The IDs in `diagnostic_15_20260929.txt` were frozen before the new Llama basic-RAG run. They are 15 of the established 50 questions and span 13 subdomains. The one Qwen baseline question with an HTTP 500 was excluded from selection.

| Failure pattern or control | Question IDs | Why included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval mismatch / missing answer concepts | bio-0097, bio-0288, bio-0403 | Ambiguous “test cross,” vessel structure, and regeneration examples were poorly covered by final evidence. |
| Terms lost between reranking and final evidence | bio-0370, bio-0380, bio-0195, bio-0239 | Separates search/ranking from passage selection or compression. |
| Baseline-to-full answer regression | bio-0061, bio-0272, bio-0006, bio-0334 | Tests whether added stages can harm answers, including when evidence concepts are present. |
| Persistently weak answers | bio-0011, bio-0317 | Misconception and immunological-memory cases. |
| Positive controls | bio-0178, bio-0091 | Multi-concept retrieval gain and numerical-correction gain. |

All answer texts below were scored again with the current `score_answer` implementation and the frozen `biology_benchmark.jsonl` keys. Means are over these exact 15 IDs. Qwen refers to the existing `aura-bio:v0` model, which is based on Qwen 3.5 4B; Llama refers to `llama3.2:1b`.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |

Llama bare → basic: 5 wins, 5 losses, 5 ties. Llama basic → full: 4 wins, 7 losses, 4 ties. These are descriptive paired counts, not a significance claim. The full configuration differs from basic in multiple stages; this comparison alone cannot isolate reranking, verification, or critique causally. Older Qwen results were produced in separate runs and three of its 15 bare answers score differently under the current scorer, which is why stored historic summary means were not reused.

The independent retrieval-only pass found 30/46 answer-key terms *not already in the question* after reranking, falling to 25/46 in the final evidence on this slice. Exact term presence is a diagnostic upper bound on answerability, not human-judged passage relevance or citation faithfulness. `bio-0097` is a clear semantic retrieval error: evidence titled “Cross-reactivity” and “Blood compatibility testing” was selected for the genetics question about a test cross. `bio-0091` illustrates a different mechanism: Llama's full score improved from 0 to 1 on the numerical Hardy–Weinberg question, while basic RAG remained at 0.

The new basic-RAG output initially recorded two Ollama HTTP 500 errors. Both questions were retried successfully; the summary uses the latest successful result for each ID and reports 15/15 answered, zero remaining errors. One basic-RAG gene-editing case spent about 374 seconds in retrieval and 18 seconds in generation. The learned router emits a scikit-learn version warning (saved under 1.7.2, loaded under 1.9.0), so router reproducibility is not perfect.

Decision rule: keep these 15 IDs fixed for targeted, single-variable diagnosis; do not reselect them based on a new result. Use the other 35 questions only for post-change validation, and a separately frozen corpus-specific set for any stronger generalization claim. Do not treat gains on this failure-enriched slice as the overall benchmark result.
